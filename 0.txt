- Current Object Type: 2D airfoil for symmetric baseline with NACA 0012
- parameterizing the object via camber, thickness distrib., and leading/trailing-edge smoothness
- AOA (Angle of Attack):-2 to +12 deg
- 5e5 to 2e6 (sample values from Reynolds Number)
- Mach number: fix to 0.2
- Geometric Constraints: min thickness >= 12%
- Leading edge radius to prevent full sharpening
- <= 12% for trailing-edge Angle
- hinge >= 20% chord
- Robust vs Narrow RL: training on envelope distribution output airfoils superior to single-condition RL
- Multifid vs Singlefid: low-fid + pretraining + high-fid fine-tuning > High-fid only (2-3x gain ideally)
- Mechanism-informed vs Generic Representation: 
parameterization from primitives such as camber/thickness coeffs, flap params, etc. have faster convergence
than splines/control points under the same dimensionality for control
